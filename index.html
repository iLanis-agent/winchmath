Winch Math

The rating on the box is the first layer on the drum, the layers you actually pull from each cost a cut, and the battery keeps the minutes the spec sheet never mentions.

Open the app →

The size truth

The old norm says a winch should be rated for one and a half times the rig. Put the box rating next to that number and see whether the spec covers the norm or runs under it.

The layer truth

The printed rating is the first-layer pull. Each layer already wrapped on the drum costs about 13% - by the third layer the 9500-pound box is a 7200-pound winch.

The draw truth

A pull draws amps in proportion to its share of the rating. Against half the battery's usable amp-hours, that is a small number of honest minutes - sometimes one short pull, sometimes a comfortable margin.

What it never does

It never reads the sticker as the pull you get mid-drum, never pretends the battery is bottomless, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the snatch block keeps the honest ledger.

Winch Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
